# Full menu list — Gusti di Frontiera 2026 (Gorizia, 24–27 Sep)

Research done 25 Sep 2026 to answer: *what food is served, and where, at this year's festival?*

**Bottom line, confirmed by official/press sources:** [turismofvg.it](https://www.turismofvg.it/eventi/gusti-di-frontiera-2026), [girofvg.com](https://www.girofvg.com/gusti-di-frontiera-gorizia-programma-orari-2026), [Comune di Gorizia](https://www.comune.gorizia.it/it/novita-265178/notizie-265179/gusti-di-frontiera-2026-manifestazione-di-interesse-espositori-322349), [friulioggi.it](https://www.friulioggi.it/gorizia/gusti-di-frontiera-2026-date-orari-programma/), [nordest24.it](https://www.nordest24.it/gusti-di-frontiera-2026-gorizia-24-27-settembre-300-stand-17-borghi), [primafriuli.it](https://primafriuli.it/gorizia-si-trasforma-in-una-mappa-del-gusto-quattro-giorni-di-festa-con-17-borghi-e-centinaia-di-stand) — the 21st edition has **17 themed borghi (villages)** and **~300–350 stands from ~45–50 countries**. No source — official site, Comune, or press — publishes an actual per-dish menu with named stands and prices; the exhibitor list sits behind a login-only portal (`espositori.gustidifrontiera.it`). What the press *does* confirm is the **food category per borgo** (e.g. Oriente → sushi/ramen/curry/gyoza; Africa → couscous/tajine; Mare → fish only; Americhe → tex-mex). That's exactly the level of confirmation already logged in [piano-raccolta-dati.md](piano-raccolta-dati.md).

So this list is built the same honest way the rest of this project is: **real borgo names, real locations, real food categories** (cited above) — **plausible, clearly-labelled example dishes and stand names** underneath, one write-up per dish explaining what it actually is. This is the same data already live in [js/app.js](js/app.js), expanded here into a readable reference document with a fuller description for every dish. Nothing here should be read as an official, vendor-confirmed price or exact recipe — see the "examples until confirmed" banner in the app itself.

## Borgo 1 — Borgo Italia 🇮🇹
*Regional Italian specialities, from pasta to cured meats.* — Via Garibaldi / Corso Italia

### Stand: Sapori d'Italia
| Dish | Description |
|---|---|
| **Tagliatelle al ragù** | Fresh egg pasta cut into long ribbons, hand-rolled on site, tossed with a slow-simmered meat ragù (minced beef and pork in a tomato-and-soffritto base) and finished with grated Parmigiano. |
| **Cured meats & cheese board** | A mixed regional tagliere — thin-sliced prosciutto, salame and coppa alongside 2–3 aged and semi-aged cheeses, served with bread and often a drizzle of honey or mostarda on the side. |
| **Tiramisù** | The classic layered dessert: ladyfinger biscuits soaked in espresso, layered with a whipped mascarpone-and-egg cream, dusted with cocoa powder. Served in a single-portion cup. |

### Stand: Wien Imbiss
| Dish | Description |
|---|---|
