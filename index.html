How to read the map

The official map already shows info points, the first-aid point, restrooms (WC), emergency exit routes, parking areas (cars, disabled, campervans) and the free mobility service. Tap a coloured number to open the matching borgo.

Emergency

For a medical or safety emergency, call the single emergency number 112. The first-aid point and emergency exit routes are marked on the official map above.
